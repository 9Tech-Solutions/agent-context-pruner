Why negative reduction occurs

At 0.95, this replay drops 7 calls, truncates 3, and loses one later-reused result. The bookkeeping notes added by pruning also take space.

The evaluator measures characters and charges an estimated full re-read for each lost result. At that threshold it reports -24% net reduction after modeled re-reads. This is a retrospective estimate, not an observed re-read or a measurement of billing or subscription usage.

This explorer shows raw threshold replays. The live Claude Code plugin falls back to built-in summarization when pruning cannot meet its configured reduction target (25% by default). That size check does not guarantee future usefulness.

What this example can tell you

These are 19 recorded thresholds from one synthetic fixture. They show aggregate outcomes, not individual call identities or a real coding task's success rate.

At 0.05, all 11 calls are kept and nothing is lost. The reuse label detects text copied later. It can miss information used to make a decision without being copied.
